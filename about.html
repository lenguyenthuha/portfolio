About Me

Hello! I'm Le Nguyen Thu Ha (often known as Smooshier). I am a student at University of Information Technology in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, focusing on AI Engineer & Data Science, alongside a passion for Photography & Visual Arts.

“The arts and sciences are avatars of human creativity."

The Dual Trajectory: Academic & Art

In my academic journey, I focus on AI, Deep Learning, and Machine Learning research — plus a soft spot for throwing ML/DL at Applied Physics problems. I find genuine joy in translating complex theoretical models into clean, efficient C++ and Python code.

Parallel to my studies, I am also a photographer and an art lover. On Behance (@lnguynthuh), I've made some graphic design projects, social media visual kits, web layouts, and creative experiments. You can also check out my photos on Instagram (@smooshier).
